# Idealization Probes

Paste when you want the agent to grill the *problem* before any solutioning. Edit the topic, send, then answer one at a time.

---

```
Grill me on this idea: {one-sentence pitch}.

Walk these five probes, one question at a time, in this order:

1. **Who** — Who is this for? Be specific about role, not title.
2. **Problem** — What's broken in the world today? Name who suffers and how.
3. **Success** — What changes if this ships? What's observable?
4. **Constraints** — What's the budget — time, scope, tech, regulatory?
5. **Ruled out** — What have I already considered and decided against?

For each probe:
- Ask the question
- Offer 2-3 plausible answers based on what you know so far
- Recommend one with a one-sentence reason
- Wait for my answer before moving on

When all five are answered, summarize back to me in the PRD template's Coverage Checklist shape and stop. Don't proceed to product requirements until I confirm.
```

---

## Why this shape

These five probes are the minimum surface to know whether to build this thing. Skipping any of them produces a PRD that papers over a hole.

The "recommend an answer" rule is the lever — it forces the agent to make a falsifiable claim instead of asking open questions you have to fill in cold.
